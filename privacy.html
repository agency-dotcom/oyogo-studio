Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains how Oyogo ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you contact us or use this website. We are the data controller for the information described below.

Who we are

Oyogo is a wellbeing travel consultancy and media platform. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at studio@oyogo.co.uk.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us:

  • Enquiry details – when you email us or use a contact link, we receive your name, email address, and anything you include in your message.
  • We do not ask for, or require, any special categories of data to make an enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently set advertising or tracking cookies, and does not run third-party analytics. If we introduce analytics or tracking in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss working together.
  • To provide the services or information you have requested.
  • To keep records of our correspondence.

Our lawful basis for processing is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business, and – where relevant – taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers that help us operate (for example, our email provider), who process data on our behalf under appropriate agreements. We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as needed to deal with your request and for our legitimate business records, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these, email studio@oyogo.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside the UK/EEA. Where they do, we take steps to ensure your data receives an appropriate level of protection.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

This policy is provided as a general starting point and should be reviewed by a qualified professional before launch to ensure it fully reflects your business and legal obligations.
